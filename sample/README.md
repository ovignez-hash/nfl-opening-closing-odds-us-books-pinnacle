### How to read it

**Prices are American odds**, as integers: `-150`, and `130` for +130. Even money is written `100`.

**Overtime counts**, in the scores and in every market, as US books settle them. A tied game has no moneyline winner: `Winner` is `Tie`.

**The spread is the home team's line.** `-3.5` means the home team gives 3.5 points; the away team's line is the opposite. Each book's spread is its main line; when a book listed several, the most balanced pair was kept.

**The total line is in points**, the same for over and under.

**A team total is the points of one team.** `Pinnacle_Team_Total_Home_Line_Close` at `24.5` is over or under 24.5 points for the home team.

**Kalshi and Polymarket are prediction markets.** Their prices are written as American odds. When the source gives no American price, the price is the cost of buying each side (the ask), converted to American odds.

**One time per book, market and phase.** The `_Time` columns are ISO 8601 in UTC and give the moment the price was recorded. Most closing prices of the US books are recorded at the same second for all of them, a few minutes before kickoff. Some come from snapshots of the market: all of BetRivers' before 2026, all of FanDuel's and DraftKings' before the 2020 season, BetMGM's of October and November 2020, and a few of the other US books' later. In all, snapshots give FanDuel 20%, DraftKings 12%, BetMGM 10%, Caesars 1%, theScore 0% of their markets. Their time is the book's last price update seen in the snapshot, which can be hours before kickoff when the book did not move. Bet365 is recorded at every change, to the second. Betcris, BetOnline's moneyline before 2020 and BetOnline's 2018 spreads come from the book's own line history, to the minute. **The opening price is the first price recorded for the game, posted at most 15 days before kickoff.** A game's line usually goes up during the week before it, two weeks ahead for the Super Bowl. Some books post lines weeks or months ahead, even for a whole season after the schedule is released in spring; DraftKings and Caesars do it for most games. Those early lines are not openings of the game week: when a book's first price is older than 15 days, its opening is left empty (4,900 markets).

**The closing price is the last pre-game price.** `Kickoff_UTC` is the scheduled kickoff; the ball is kicked a few minutes later and books take bets until then. Median closing time on the moneyline, against the scheduled kickoff: Pinnacle at the start, Bet365 7 min before, FanDuel 9 min before, DraftKings 9 min before, BetMGM 9 min before, Caesars 9 min before, BetRivers 70 min before, BetOnline 9 min before, theScore 9 min before, Fanatics at the start, Hard Rock at the start, Betcris 7 min before, Kalshi 9 min before, Polymarket 1 min before. No closing price is older than 12 hours before the scheduled kickoff, and none is stamped more than 5 minutes after it, or 30 minutes for playoff games: their kickoff comes later after the scheduled time, so a playoff closing price stamped more than 5 minutes after it was kept only when it matched the market before kickoff (within 0.03 in no-vig implied probability on the moneyline, 1.5 points on the spread and the total). In the market snapshots, a book's last update stamped after the scheduled kickoff is an in-game price: none of these 9,265 updates was used.

**Closing prices were checked against the market at kickoff.** A closing price was compared with the median of the other books' closing prices recorded from one hour before kickoff to 5 minutes after it, on the same game, and left out when it was more than 0.05 away in no-vig implied probability on the moneyline, 3 points or more away on the spread, or 2 points or more away on the total. When a book's first source failed that check and its other source passed it, the other source was used, opening price included (9 markets); otherwise the closing price was left out (53 prices).

**Opening prices were checked against the openings posted at the same time.** In the NFL, books open days apart and lines move hard on injury news, so an opening price is compared only with the other books' openings posted within 12 hours of it. It was left out (38 prices) when it was more than 0.15 away from them in no-vig implied probability on the moneyline, or 4 points or more away on the spread or the total, unless it already matched the market at the close. It was also left out when its spread had the sign opposite to the other openings (8 prices), or when it was more than 0.45 away from the market at the close on the moneyline, or 15 points or more on the spread or the total (1 price).

- **The file starts with the 2018 season**, on 6 September 2018.
- **Pinnacle starts with the 2020 season**, on 10 September 2020. Its team totals run from 3 January 2021 to 8 February 2026; the 2026 season has none yet. 78 team totals were left out: their sum was more than 4 points away from the game total, their difference more than 4 points away from the spread, or the other team had none.
- **Bet365's totals start on 13 October 2019**; its moneyline and spread start with the 2018 season. **Bet365 has no price on the 28 games of January 2026** (week 18 of the 2025 season and the playoffs up to the conference championships): its record holds none for that month.
- **FanDuel's closing prices start on 4 October 2018**, its opening prices with the 2019 season. **DraftKings has 1 game of the 2018 season** (Super Bowl LIII), then every season from 2019. Its opening prices are often empty because it posts most lines months ahead: 15% of the 2023 to 2025 games have a DraftKings opening moneyline. Caesars does the same with its spreads (23% of those games have a Caesars opening spread).
- **BetMGM and Caesars start with the 2020 season.** BetMGM's prices of October and November 2020 come from market snapshots only: the other record carried unusable spreads and totals in those two months.
- **BetRivers' prices before 2026 come from market snapshots**, closing a median 70 minutes before kickoff, with few opening prices (11 moneylines, 9 spreads and 61 totals).
- **The 2025 season has almost nothing for BetRivers, Fanatics and Hard Rock** (1, 6 and 3 closing prices): their record that season is market snapshots, almost all stamped after kickoff: in-game prices that were not used. Fanatics and Hard Rock are on the 2026 games; Fanatics' closing moneyline starts on 25 October 2026.
- **BetOnline has the moneyline from the 2018 season, the spread in 2018 and from 2020, the total from 2020.**
- **Betcris covers every season except 2021**, the 2026 season through 2 November 2026.
- **theScore starts on 16 November 2023.**
- **Kalshi carries closing prices from 16 November 2025 and Polymarket from 10 January 2026**, both with opening prices from 22 October 2026. In 2026, their closing prices are identical in 97 of the 129 games where both have one.
- **Bet365's moneyline closes a median 7 minutes before kickoff.** It has no closing moneyline on 92 games: the 28 games of January 2026, 54 games where its last moneyline change came more than 12 hours before kickoff, and 10 games its record does not have.
- **When the opening record is the closing record itself (same moment), it appears once, as the closing price** (1,764 cases). An opening price equal to the closing price at a different time is kept: the book did not move. No opening price is stamped after the scheduled kickoff. A book's opening and closing prices in a market always come from the same source. 1,336 markets have an opening price and no closing price.
- **Prices that cannot exist were not used**: moneylines with less than 1.5% margin (the two sides were not taken at the same moment) or more than 25%, closing moneylines with more than 10% margin, spread, total and team total prices adding up to less than 100% or more than 115% or beyond ±250, prediction-market prices whose two sides do not add up, and lines out of range. 1 spread giving the points to the moneyline favourite was removed.
- **The 2026 season stops after week 10**, 150 games. Later weeks come with the monthly update.
